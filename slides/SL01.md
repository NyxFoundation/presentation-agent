# AI agents are becoming autonomous economic actors, but rigorous research on their real-stakes behavior is virtually nonexistent.

- AI agent market: $7.8B (2025) → $52.6B (2030), 46% CAGR (MarketsandMarkets)
- Agentic commerce projected at $300-500B in U.S. retail by 2030 (Bain)
- University IRBs lack expertise for AI economic experiments; real-asset studies blocked
- Corporate AI governance focused on liability avoidance, not exploratory research
- Virtually no peer-reviewed research on AI-to-AI autonomous economic transactions with real financial stakes (based on Google Scholar and arXiv search as of January 2025)
