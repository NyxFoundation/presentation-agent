# Japan's cryptography expertise remains world-class, yet our global research influence has steadily declined.

- CRYPTREC: Japan maintains cryptographic evaluation infrastructure comparable to NESSIE (EU) and NIST AES (US)
- ASIACRYPT: Japanese researchers founded Asia's first major cryptography conference in 1990—now one of the world's three leading venues
- NISTEP 2025: Japan dropped to 13th globally in Top 10% cited papers, down from 4th in the late 1990s
- Research output share: declined from 7.4% to 4.7% between 2005-2015
