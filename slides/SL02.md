# While global research investment grew 80%, Japan's stagnation has eroded the collaborative infrastructure that once made us formidable.

- 20-year funding gap: US and Germany increased university research spending 80%; Japan increased only 10%
- ¥140 billion cumulative reduction in national university operating grants since 2004
- 2024 Kakenhi campaign: 12 major academic associations (including Science Council of Japan sections and Mathematical Society of Japan) demanded doubled funding (from ¥240B to ¥480B)
- Research time erosion: faculty research time fell from 46.5% (2002) to 37.2% (2008), unchanged through 2018
