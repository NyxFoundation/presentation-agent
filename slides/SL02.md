# AI agents are becoming economic actors, but policymakers and educators lack empirical data to guide them

- Current state: AI agents increasingly perform autonomous transactions (booking, purchasing, negotiating) in real-world contexts
- Research gap: Near-zero academic papers study AI agent economy with real-asset stakes—most research is simulation-only
- Root cause: University ethics boards block crypto-based AI experiments; corporate accounting rules prohibit holding/distributing crypto
- Opportunity: First-mover window exists before regulatory frameworks lock in (2025-2027)
- 2040 alignment: Research findings directly inform curriculum for human-AI coexistence—core to Sony Global Education's mission
