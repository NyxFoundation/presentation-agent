# Four risks you can't test by handing AI authority in production

<div class="rk4">
  <div class="rk4-card">
    <div class="rk4-icon">1</div>
    <div class="rk4-h">Short-term Profit Bias</div>
    <div class="rk4-n">AI chooses actions that maximize immediate profit over long-term safety.<br/><span class="rk4-ex">e.g. concentrating funds in high-risk, high-return arbitrage or lending</span></div>
  </div>
  <div class="rk4-card">
    <div class="rk4-icon">2</div>
    <div class="rk4-h">Privilege Overreach</div>
    <div class="rk4-n">AI moves assets, calls contracts, or changes rules beyond its granted scope.<br/><span class="rk4-ex">e.g. cascading access into unintended APIs or external systems</span></div>
  </div>
  <div class="rk4-card">
    <div class="rk4-icon">3</div>
    <div class="rk4-h">Rule Loopholes</div>
    <div class="rk4-n">AI exploits spec gaps and conditional-branch boundaries to extract profit.<br/><span class="rk4-ex">e.g. timing gaps, rounding errors, priority-order loopholes</span></div>
  </div>
  <div class="rk4-card">
    <div class="rk4-icon">4</div>
    <div class="rk4-h">Unauditability</div>
    <div class="rk4-n">Why AI made a given decision, and which logs would let us reproduce or verify it, is unclear.<br/><span class="rk4-ex">e.g. unable to trace "who" and "why" after an incident</span></div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="rk4-evidence">
  <div class="rk4-ev-eye">Domain where the same class of risk has already materialized</div>

</div>

<!--
Speaker Notes:
Before putting AI agents into production, there are four risks you must test for in advance. (1) Short-term profit bias — AI prefers actions with high immediate gain over long-term safety. (2) Privilege overreach — AI moves assets, calls contracts, or changes rules beyond its granted scope. (3) Rule loopholes — AI exploits spec boundaries and conditional-branch gaps for profit. (4) Unauditability — you can't trace why AI made a decision or which logs would let you reproduce and verify it. These are AI-agent-specific risks, but the same structural damage is already occurring in DeFi at roughly $3.4 billion per year — the result of economic attacks, rule loopholes, and automated profit maximization combined. When AI agents expand into broader business domains, the same risk structure will spread beyond finance. The blast radius is too large to fix after the fact in production. You need a place to fail safely beforehand.
