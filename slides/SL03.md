# Why Ethereum?

<div class="text-xs opacity-50 -mt-2 mb-3">What draws Nyx is not cryptocurrency, but the potential of a "digital infrastructure where truth can be verified"</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4">

<div class="p-4 border border-gray-200 rounded-lg">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-2 mb-2">
    <div class="text-xl">🔎</div>
    <div class="font-bold text-sm">Because what happened can be verified afterward</div>
  </div>
  <div class="text-[10px] opacity-60 leading-relaxed mb-2">
    Rather than simply trusting someone's explanation, records, execution results, and change histories can be verified by third parties.<br/>
    This becomes the "foundation of trust" essential for research, auditing, and public systems.
  </div>
  <div class="flex gap-2">
    <div class="text-[9px] px-2 py-0.5 bg-black text-white rounded">Verifiability</div>
    <div class="text-[9px] px-2 py-0.5 bg-gray-100 rounded">Transparency</div>
    <div class="text-[9px] px-2 py-0.5 bg-gray-100 rounded">Tamper Resistance</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="p-4 border border-gray-200 rounded-lg">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-2 mb-2">
    <div class="text-xl">🛡️</div>
    <div class="font-bold text-sm">Because society's hardest problems appear here first</div>
  </div>
  <div class="text-[10px] opacity-60 leading-relaxed mb-2">
    Post-quantum migration, security auditing, market fairness—challenges that many social infrastructures will eventually face already exist as real problems on Ethereum.
  </div>
  <div class="flex gap-2">
    <div class="text-[9px] px-2 py-0.5 bg-black text-white rounded">PQ Migration</div>
    <div class="text-[9px] px-2 py-0.5 bg-gray-100 rounded">Security</div>
    <div class="text-[9px] px-2 py-0.5 bg-gray-100 rounded">Market Design</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="p-4 border border-gray-200 rounded-lg">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-2 mb-2">
    <div class="text-xl">🌱</div>
    <div class="font-bold text-sm">Because even small teams can reach the world</div>
  </div>
  <div class="text-[10px] opacity-60 leading-relaxed mb-2">
    Because specifications, implementations, and discussions are open, people are evaluated by their output rather than credentials or affiliations.<br/>
    Young researchers and independent teams can directly contribute to the world's most important challenges.
  </div>
  <div class="flex gap-2">
    <div class="text-[9px] px-2 py-0.5 bg-black text-white rounded">Open Science</div>
    <div class="text-[9px] px-2 py-0.5 bg-gray-100 rounded">Meritocracy</div>
    <div class="text-[9px] px-2 py-0.5 bg-gray-100 rounded">Global Collaboration</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="p-4 border border-gray-200 rounded-lg">
  <div class="flex items-center gap-2 mb-2">
    <div class="text-xl">🏠</div>
    <div class="font-bold text-sm">Because we can build a place where talent gathers and grows</div>
  </div>
  <div class="text-[10px] opacity-60 leading-relaxed mb-2">
    Ethereum has room for researchers, developers, and individuals to discuss, learn from each other, and improve together beyond institutional boundaries.<br/>
    Nyx aims to sustainably cultivate this environment from Japan.
  </div>
  <div class="flex gap-2">
    <div class="text-[9px] px-2 py-0.5 bg-black text-white rounded">Talent Development</div>
    <div class="text-[9px] px-2 py-0.5 bg-gray-100 rounded">Joint Research</div>
    <div class="text-[9px] px-2 py-0.5 bg-gray-100 rounded">Community</div>
  </div>
</div>

</div>

<div class="mt-3 text-center text-[10px] opacity-50">
  Invention is born from open questions. Ethereum is where those questions converge most densely.
</div>

<div class="mt-2 text-center text-[10px] opacity-40">
  * Ethereum: An open digital infrastructure where participants worldwide share records and execution under common rules
</div>
