# SGE's learner-centered mission requires understanding AI agent behavior under real incentives

- **Isozu:** "AI can do what AI is good at, humans should do what AI cannot"—AI shifts education from memorization to evaluation
- **SGE needs:** empirical data on agent behavior to fulfill learner-centered mission
- **Gap:** no auditable testbed exists to study agent behaviors that affect learner interactions
