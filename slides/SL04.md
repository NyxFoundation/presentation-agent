# The proposal: 100+ teams, AI agents trading on private blockchain, L0/L1/L2 research framework

- Competition format: 100+ teams develop AI agents that trade custom tokens on a private blockchain—research sandbox, not public crypto
- Research framework: L0 (execution/blockchain) → L1 (institutions/rules) → L2 (AI agents)—each layer can be experimentally manipulated
- Human-in-the-loop: Human researchers monitor in real-time and can intervene; agents operate within pre-defined constraints
- Educational output: Experimental data feeds directly into curriculum design for AI ethics and economic literacy
