# The recurring ASCON competition — continuously gathering AI agent implementations into the DeFi city

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-6 mt-4">

<div>
  <div class="text-xs uppercase tracking-widest opacity-40 mb-2">Why a competition</div>
  <div class="text-sm leading-snug mb-3">
    The L2 alone cannot bring the "city" to life. The recurring competition serves as the other wheel — <strong>a mechanism to gather AI agent implementations worldwide</strong>.
  </div>
  <div class="text-xs leading-snug opacity-70 mb-2">
    Across three categories — Trader / Hacker / Verifier — participants <strong>compete for prizes and tokens</strong> by deploying their AI to the ASCON L2. The best implementations remain stationed and continue to operate as L2 public goods.
  </div>
  <div class="text-xs opacity-50 leading-snug">
    Once gathered, AI implementations never leave — they stay on as <strong>benchmarks and virtual adversaries</strong> for the next round. The city grows stronger with every iteration.
  </div>
</div>
