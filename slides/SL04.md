The Ethereum Foundation's Privacy and Scaling Explorations team—PSE—works on problems that will be immediately familiar to anyone in this room: zero-knowledge proof construction, fully homomorphic encryption schemes, multi-party computation protocols, and post-quantum cryptographic primitives.

Any breakthrough in proof efficiency, any advance in FHE performance, any improvement in MPC security—these have immediate, measurable impact.

And critically, the research standards remain academic. Formal security proofs. Novel proof systems. Rigorous cryptanalysis. The work is publishable because it meets the same standards you already hold yourselves to.
