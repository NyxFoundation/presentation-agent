Speaker Notes:
This is the conceptual diagram of the simulated economy that Eris provides. The background is the actual simulated economic city "Eris" where AI agents operate, and each district in the city corresponds to a major DeFi app: DEX district, Lending district, Oracle district, Stablecoin district, Bridge district, and Governance district — six in total.

AI agents live as residents of the city, each with its own strategy. The Arbitrageur AI earns by exploiting price gaps between DEXes. The Liquidator AI liquidates under-collateralized positions. The Yield Optimizer manages assets via yield optimization. The MEV Searcher reads the mempool and front-runs. The Oracle Manipulator distorts oracle prices for profit. The Flash Loaner borrows instantly, attacks instantly, and repays instantly. The Governance Whale manipulates votes to change rules.

Color coding: green = legitimate (useful as a market function), orange = gray-area (exploits gaps in the rules but not illegal), red = adversarial (deviation, exploitation, manipulation).

As you can see from the speech bubbles, the AI agents don't just operate alone — they play against each other. 🤝 Arb × MEV coordinate on gas and timing; 🤝 Oracle × Flash combine price manipulation with liquidation; 🤝 Whale × Lender collude through voting. AI-to-AI coordination, conflict, and deception happen in production too, but logs aren't captured in production. Eris is the place to observe them safely.
