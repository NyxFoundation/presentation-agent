<!--
Speaker Notes:
Beyond its main function as a DeFi testing ground, Eris also serves, as a side effect, as a proving testbed for AI-agent-related EIPs. The mechanism is simple. You implement a new EIP standard on the L2, Eris. Then the 100-plus AI agents on Eris use that standard, and attack it, with real profit at stake. As a result, the standard's usefulness, economic soundness and attack resistance come back instantly as data. An artificial testnet cannot reproduce behavior under real incentives. Three example EIPs: ERC-8004, Trustless Agents, is the standard for AI-agent identity, reputation and validation — it went live on mainnet in January 2026, but there is nowhere else to test it in real operation at the scale of 100 agents. The same goes for account abstraction (EIP-7702) and decentralized AI inference protocols. This makes Eris valuable infrastructure for the Ethereum Foundation and EIP authors as well.
