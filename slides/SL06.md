# The Ethereum Foundation Academic Grants program funds 41 projects annually with dedicated support for cryptography, formal verification, and security research.

- 2025 Academic Grants Round: $2 million dedicated fund, proposals due March 16, 2025
- 2024 results: 41 projects funded from global applicants across 25+ countries
- Research domains: Applied/Theoretical Cryptography, Formal Verification, Consensus, Security, Economics
- Project timelines: 6-12 months, designed to fit academic schedules; open-access output required
