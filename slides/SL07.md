# Modular tiers and exit gates let each entity engage at their comfort level

- **4 tiers:** Observer → Advisor → Co-Designer → Sponsor (increasing involvement, not lock-in)
- **Ownership:** each entity designates one owner; Nyx Foundation handles day-to-day operations
- **Exit gates:** defined per phase—each transition requires explicit sign-off to continue
