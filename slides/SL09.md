Speaker Notes:
There are four sponsor tiers at fixed prices: $3.5k, $15k, $20k, and $35k. Tiers are not chosen by industry, but by "what you want to get." The $3.5k Brand Sponsor — internal approval name "AI Agent Risk Verification Program Brand Sponsorship" — is for companies that only want logo placement and talent touchpoints. It includes the LP, event logo, and participant data connection. No technical articles, prizes, or theme-setting rights. Best for recruiting branding or technical PR. The $15k Challenge Partner — approval name "AI Agent Risk Verification Theme Sponsorship" — gets theme sponsorship, sponsor prize, theme-setting, judges, early access to behavior-log summaries, and 1 technical article. Behavior logs are released publicly later, but plans $15k and above get early access. Best for companies that want to be visible in the AI risk conversation. The $20k Product Integration Partner — approval name "AI Agent Product Integration PoC" — is the core plan. Embed your product/API into the AI agent execution environment and get early access to deep analysis of relevant scope, 2-3 technical articles, demo video, and top-team interviews. The $35k Strategic Partner — approval name "Agentic AI Risk Simulation Strategic Sponsorship" — embeds the sponsor deeply as a title sponsor of the experimental environment. Strategic Partners get exclusive access to summaries of top submissions, anonymized logs, replay artifacts, and sponsor-relevant analysis, based on participant disclosure tier and individual consent. This is not unrestricted third-party provision of unpublished code or prompts. Includes joint white paper or joint publication. Pricing is designed not as a flashy event fee, but as the core cost of producing implementation artifacts and behavior logs.
