# DeFi is the lab environment for observing AI agents' economic behavior

<div class="wd-intro">
  An environment where prices, assets, contracts, permissions, external data, and competitors all move simultaneously simply <b>doesn't exist outside DeFi</b> in the real world.
  By letting AI agents compete there, you can safely preview the kind of dangerous optimization that would occur in your production environment.
</div>
