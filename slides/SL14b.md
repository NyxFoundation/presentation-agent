# Research the competition produces

<div class="rt3">

  <div class="rt-col rt-col-sure">
    <div class="rt-when rt-when-sure">Certain</div>
    <div class="rt-cond">Outputs the competition itself produces</div>

      <div class="rt-h">A new security baseline</div>
      <div class="rt-n">From the Hacker × Verifier match data, publish <strong>DeFi design patterns and benchmarks that withstand dynamic, economic attacks</strong>.</div>
