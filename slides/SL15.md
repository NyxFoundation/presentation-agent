Speaker Notes:
Evaluation splits into three tracks. The Strategy Track scores trading and optimization: Profit 50%, Risk-adjusted Return 20%, Capital Efficiency 10%, Stability 10%, Explainability 10%. The Exploit Discovery Track scores vulnerability hunting: Validity 35%, Impact 25%, Novelty 15%, Reproducibility 15%, Report Quality 10%. The Verification Track scores proof and verification: Correctness 30%, Coverage 20%, Evidence Quality 20%, Low False Positives 15%, Actionability 15%. On top of these, the Sponsor Insight View carries independent perspectives — detection quality, evidence quality, policy mapping, insight reusability — that measure the effect of the sponsor product. The key is the two-layer structure: the competition's energy is created by the Trader / Hacker / Verifier's profit, discovery, and proof, while the value needed for internal-approval purposes is surfaced as risk, control, audit, and reproducibility.
