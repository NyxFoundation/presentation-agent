<!--
Speaker Notes:
On top of its primary function as a DeFi test platform, Eris also serves as an empirical testbed for AI-agent-related EIPs. The mechanism is simple. Implement a new EIP standard onto Eris, an L2. 100+ AI agents on Eris then use and attack that standard with real profit at stake. As a result, the standard's usefulness, economic soundness, and attack resistance become available as data instantly. Artificial testnets can't reproduce behavior under real incentives. Three example EIPs: ERC-8004 (Trustless Agents) is a standard for AI-agent identity, reputation, and verification — it went live on mainnet in January 2026, but no other venue can run production-style tests with 100+ agents. Account-abstraction EIP-7702 and decentralized AI inference protocols are similar. This makes Eris valuable infrastructure for the Ethereum Foundation and EIP authors as well.
