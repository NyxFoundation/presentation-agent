Speaker Notes:
To estimate ASCON's participant scale, we compare competitions with similar prize sizes and fields.

Kaggle's major contests at $50K-$1M attract 1,000-5,000 teams, but that's the power of a dedicated platform and huge community.

NeurIPS's Competition Track at $25K-$100K is in the 100-500 team range, more technical and academic.

ETHGlobal's hackathon at $300K-$1M reaches 1,000-2,500 people — a major event in the Web3 industry.

AICrowd's Lux AI Challenge 2 at $50K drew 1,178 teams — this is the closest comparable to ASCON.s prize scale ($70k), in the multi-agent space.

ASCON sits at the triple intersection of AI x DeFi x security, so technical barriers are higher, but the narrative (AI agentic safety) is strong right now and we can aim for reach beyond Lux AI.

Base estimate: 300 teams; conservative 200; upside 500+. Participant counts swing widely based on more than prize money — announcement partners, narrative, and submission overhead (ASCON is a simple single-zip submission) all matter.
